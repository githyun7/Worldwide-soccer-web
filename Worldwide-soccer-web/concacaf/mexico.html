Mexico is the most successful national team in CONCACAF history, known for its technical skill, creativity, and passionate fanbase. The team has been a consistent presence at the FIFA World Cup, often advancing beyond the group stage.

Liga MX is one of the strongest leagues in the Americas and plays a vital role in developing elite talent. Mexican soccer culture is deeply ingrained in national identity, with matches drawing massive support both domestically and internationally.

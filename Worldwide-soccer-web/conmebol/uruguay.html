Uruguay is a historic football powerhouse, winning the first-ever FIFA World Cup in 1930 and again in 1950. The country has a strong Copa America record with 15 titles and is famous for producing world-class players like Luis Suárez and Diego Forlán.

Uruguay’s domestic league, the Primera División Uruguaya, continues to nurture talented players. The nation remains an influential football country, known for its tactical discipline, technical skill, and passion for the game.

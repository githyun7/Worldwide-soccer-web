FIFA YOUTH WORLD CUP

Age GroupUnder-20 / Under-17
First Held1977
Famous AlumniMessi, Mbappé, Neymar
Current ChampionMorocco U-20

The FIFA Youth World Cup is an international tournament designed to showcase and develop young soccer talent from around the world. Organized in age categories such as Under-17 and Under-20, the competition provides emerging players with valuable experience on the global stage. It serves as an important stepping stone between youth football and senior international competition.

Throughout its history, the Youth World Cup has introduced future superstars including Lionel Messi, Kylian Mbappé, Neymar, and Ronaldinho, many of whom later became legends of the sport. The tournament highlights raw talent, creativity, and tactical growth, offering fans a glimpse into the future of world soccer.

In addition to player development, the FIFA Youth World Cup strengthens international cooperation and youth training systems across continents. It encourages nations to invest in academies, coaching education, and long-term player development strategies. By nurturing young athletes at an early stage, the tournament plays a vital role in shaping the next generation of global soccer stars.

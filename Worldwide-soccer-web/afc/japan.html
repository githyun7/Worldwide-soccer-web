Japan is the most successful national team in the history of the AFC Asian Cup and a model for long-term soccer development in Asia. Since the creation of the professional J-League in the 1990s, Japan has focused on technical excellence, youth development, and structured coaching systems.

Japanese players are known for their discipline, tactical intelligence, and teamwork. The national team consistently competes against top nations at the FIFA World Cup, earning global respect for its ability to challenge traditionally stronger soccer countries.

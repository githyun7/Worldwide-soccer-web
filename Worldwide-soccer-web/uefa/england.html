England is the birthplace of modern soccer and home to the world-renowned Premier League. The national team, known as the Three Lions, has a rich history of competing at the highest level in international tournaments. England's domestic league has produced countless top players who have excelled globally.

The national team is characterized by strong tactical play, physicality, and a passionate fanbase. England continues to compete at every World Cup and European Championship, demonstrating its ongoing influence and importance in world football.
